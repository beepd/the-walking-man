Privacy Policy

Short version: this site does not ask you for any personal information, and does not have accounts, sign-ups or comments.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What we collect

The Walking Man itself does not collect, store or send your name, email address or any other personal details. The page works out where the man is from your device’s clock, so it needs no server and does not track you.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it may keep standard technical logs (for example your IP address and the page you asked for) for security and to run the service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for how they handle this.

Sound

The sounds (footsteps, waves, crickets, fire) are created in your browser. Nothing is recorded, and the site never uses your microphone. Sound starts only after you tap or press a key.

Advertising and cookies

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technology to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or learn more at how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • In regions such as the European Economic Area and the UK, you will be asked for your consent to cookies before personalised ads are shown.

We do not control the cookies set by advertisers, and we do not receive any personal information from them.

Children

The site is made to be suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
